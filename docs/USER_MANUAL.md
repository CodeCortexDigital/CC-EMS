# CC-EMS User Manual: where is what

For the people who use the system every day: administrators, HR, teachers, parents and students.
It tells you **where to click** for each job. It does not explain how the software is built.

> Menu names below are the ones on screen. A person only sees the menu items their role is allowed to use,
> so your menu may be shorter than the lists here.

---

## 1. Signing in

1. Open the school's web address and choose **Admin**, **Staff** or **Student / Parent**.
2. Type your username (an email address, or the ID your school gave you) and your password, then press **Sign in**.
3. **Forgot password?** is under the password box.

**Demo school.** The demo site shows four ready-made accounts under the sign-in button. Click one to fill it in.

| Role | Username | Password |
|---|---|---|
| Admin | admin@code.com | Admin@123 |
| Teacher | teacher@code.com | Teacher@123 |
| Parent | parent@code.com | Parent@123 |
| Student | student@code.com | Student@123 |

The demo runs on a free server that goes to sleep when nobody uses it. **The first sign-in after a quiet period can
take up to a minute.** If you see "timeout", wait a moment and press Sign in again.

## 1a. The first-time tour, and installing the app

* At each sign-in a short **tour** shows where things are for your role, until you have been through every step and pressed **Finish**. **Skip** closes it for the rest of that sign-in only; it comes back the next time you sign in. Use **Take the tour** on **Help & support** to see it again after finishing.
* **Install the app.** In **Account Settings**, **App and notifications** lets you install the portal on your phone or computer. On an iPhone or iPad, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
* **Notifications.** Turn them on for a device to get alerts when the app is closed, choose which kinds you want (attendance, finance, exams and so on), send yourself a test, and remove old devices.

## 2. Finding your way around

* **Left menu (sidebar):** the main areas. It is grouped under headings (People, Academics, Billing, and so on). Click a heading to open or close it.
* **Tab bar across the top of a page:** the pages inside an area. For example, inside **Fees** you get *Invoices, Fee Collection, Family Accounts*.
* **Search (top bar):** opens a quick finder to jump to a page.
* **Messages, Announcements, Calendar, Meetings:** in the menu for every role.
* **Account Settings, then Log out:** at the bottom of the menu. Change your own password under Account Settings.

### 3.2 Admissions

**Admissions** has five tabs: **Applications**, **Waiting list**, **Tours & interviews**, **Re-enrolment** and **Online form settings**.

* **Applications** move through: Pending, Reviewing, Approved, Waitlisted, Rejected, Enrolled, Withdrawn. Click a status to filter. Open an application to see its documents checklist and, if the school charges one, its application fee.
* **Online form settings** has the public link families use to apply, the list of documents to ask for, and the **application fee** (leave it empty for no fee). While a fee is unpaid the application is **on hold**: it cannot be reviewed until the fee is paid, recorded at the office, or waived with a reason.
* **Waiting list.** Click **Set seats for a grade** (for example Grade 5, 2026-2027, 20 seats). Add waitlisted applications to that grade's list. The line goes: families with a child already at the school, then staff children, then whoever joined first. When a seat is free (a place is withdrawn or declined, or you add seats) the first family is **offered the seat** by email with a deadline. If they decline or run out of time, the next family is offered it automatically. You can see open offers and take one back with **Withdraw**.
* **Tours & interviews.** **Add a time** (tour or interview, how many families it fits). Families book one from the link in their emails; they are reminded the day before. Booking an interview also sets the application's interview date.
* **Documents checklist** (inside an application). Each required document shows Missing, Waiting for you to check, Received or Sent back. Press **Received**, or **Send again...** with a reason: the family is emailed and can upload a new file. Families who still owe documents get a weekly reminder. When you enrol the student, their documents move onto the student's record.
* **New application** is for entering one by hand (a phone call or a paper form).
* **Re-enrolment** is for current families confirming their place for next year.

**What families see.** The emails they receive link to **Your application** (`/apply/manage`), where, with only their application number and tracking code, they can accept or decline a seat, pay the application fee by card, upload documents and book a tour or interview.

### 3.4 Leave and substitute cover

1. A teacher requests leave: **Timetable**, **My Leave**.
2. Admin or HR approves it: **Timetable**, **Staff Leave**.
3. When leave is approved, the system looks at that teacher's lessons **on the dates they are away** and offers each one to a free teacher who is **qualified for that subject** and under their daily and weekly limit.
4. If nobody qualified is free, the lesson stays as an **open cover request**. HR then assigns someone, or a free colleague can press **I can cover**. Both are under **Staff & HR**, **Cover Requests**.
5. A one-off absence (a sick call in the morning) does not need a leave request. Use **Staff & HR**, **Cover Requests** to report the absence for a date, and the system finds cover the same way. Approved leave is covered automatically; Cover Requests is for one-off absences, swaps and anything left over.

Limits (days allowed per leave type, such as sick, personal, bereavement, jury duty) are set by the admin in **Timetable**, **Leave Limits**.

### 3.5 Timetable and teaching rules

* **Teaching Rules** (admin, in the Timetable area): say which subjects each teacher is qualified for, the most periods a day or week, and the most in a row. The generator, manual edits and cover all follow these rules. The page also lists subjects nobody can teach and teachers with no subjects, so fix those first.
* **Time Periods, Class Rooms, Weekdays:** the building blocks of the timetable.
* **Create Timetable:** builds the week for all classes automatically, using only qualified teachers. You can change any slot by hand afterwards. A change that breaks a rule (wrong subject, teacher already busy) is refused with a reason.
* **Generate For Class / Generate For Teacher:** print or view one class or one teacher.

**Generating invoices.** Open **Fees**, **Generate Invoices**. Choose the class and month, check the fee items that are ticked

student and month, so you cannot create empty or double bills by accident.

### 3.7a Front desk, health and safety

These tools are for administrators and for staff given a named role: **front desk**, **nurse** or **counsellor** (create a role with that name and give it to the person; they land on their own tool when they sign in).

**Front Desk** (administrators and the front desk role) has two tabs:

* **Pickup.** Find the student. You see the parents and guardians who may collect them, and any other authorised adults. Press **Release**, tick that you checked their photo ID, add a reason, and save. Add someone with **Add an authorised adult**. A person marked **DO NOT RELEASE** (with the court-order note) cannot be released by the front desk; only an administrator can, by typing a reason that is recorded in the activity log. Every release is kept and cannot be edited.
* **Visitors.** Sign visitors in (name, who they visit, purpose, badge number) and out. **On site now** is the list for an emergency; **Print on-site list** prints it.

**Health Office** (administrators and the nurse). Find a student, then:

* **Visits:** **New visit** records time in and out, the complaint, what was done and whether the student went home. The parents are told automatically.
* **Medication:** **Add medication** records what a parent has allowed (medicine, dose, times, dates, and the hours that must pass between doses). Doses can only be given against a current, consented permission. If a dose is too soon after the last one you must type a reason. Doses are never edited afterwards.
* Immunisations have a **next due date** on the student's Health tab; parents get one reminder per due date.
* Health records are private: only the nurse, administrators and the child's own parents can see them, and every view is written to the activity log.

**Safety Reports** (everyone on staff can file one; administrators and the counsellor see all). **Report an incident** with what happened, where, the students involved, and whether to tell their parents. Reviewers add **follow-up tasks** with an owner and a due date (overdue ones are flagged at the top) and **close** the incident with a note. A closed incident cannot be changed until it is reopened. Reports are never deleted.

**Forms** (administrators). **New form**: the text families agree to, optional questions (yes/no, short answer, choose one), who it goes to (every family or chosen classes) and a sign-by date. Save as a draft, then **Publish**: each child's family gets a form to sign and a notification. **Who has signed** shows signed, declined and waiting, lets you **remind** only those who have not signed, and downloads a CSV. A signed form's wording cannot change; use **New version** instead.

### 3.7b Interventions, absence steps, AI help and planning

**Interventions** (administrators and the counsellor; a teacher or staff member sees **My Interventions**, the ones given to them). A list of students who need support. They open on their own when a student's unexcused absences reach a step of your absence ladder, or when the early-warning scan flags a student. You can also add one by hand with **New intervention**. Open one to see why it opened and suggested next steps, give it an **owner** and a due date, add notes, and press **Mark done** (you must say what was done) or **Dismiss** (you must say why). The tiles at the top show open, overdue and unassigned ones. A closed one can be reopened. An owner can add notes and finish their own interventions but not reassign them.

**Absence steps** (Attendance, then Absence Reports). Set what happens as unexcused absences add up. The usual ladder is 3, 5 and 10 absences. Each step warns the family, and a step can also tell staff and open an intervention. Excused absences never count. **Back to the usual steps** restores 3, 5 and 10. **School Reports, Attendance** also shows **Chronic absenteeism**: students who miss 10% or more of the days recorded (students with fewer than 10 days recorded are left out).

**AI Assistant** (Settings, administrators). Switch each AI feature on or off for your school, see this month's use, and accept the **AI use notice**. Features that send a student's name to the AI provider (such as drafting a message to a family) will not work until the notice is accepted. Everything the AI writes is a **draft**: it is never sent, and it never decides a grade, an admission or discipline.

**Daily Briefs** (administrators only; second item in the menu). One page for the morning and one for the evening, instead of looking everywhere. Counts and amounts only: no student or staff names appear on them or are ever shown to the AI.

* **Morning brief** (ready about 6:30 a.m. by default): safety incidents still open and overdue follow-ups, who is on leave and the lessons still needing cover, student interventions overdue or without an owner, students on roll, yesterday's attendance, late arrivals and behaviour incidents, fees overdue, new applications, school tours booked today, and waiting-list offers about to expire, expiring staff credentials, and what is on today's calendar.
* **Evening summary** (about 5:00 p.m.): attendance today, behaviour incidents, absence-ladder steps triggered, incidents reported and closed, health office visits, medication doses given (and any refused, missed or in error), forms signed, visitors still signed in, cover left unfilled, interventions opened and completed, payments received, and what needs doing tomorrow.
* **Needs attention** is listed first, with a link to the screen that deals with it. Safety and health come before staff, then student support, attendance, admissions and money.
* **Nothing is final until you approve it.** Each brief is made as a draft with a short written summary. Read it, change the words if you like, then press **Approve**. An approved brief is locked (it can be printed or deleted, not changed) and records who approved it and when. If AI is switched on the summary is AI-written from the figures; otherwise it is a plain summary of the same figures, and the page says which.
* **Today** has **Prepare it now** (or **Prepare again** while it is still a draft). **Daily history** lists the days kept. **Weekly, fortnightly, monthly** builds a roll-up from the daily briefs (**Build the latest weekly brief** and so on) and works the same way: draft, edit, approve, print or delete.
* **Headlines or full detail.** Every brief has a **Headlines** button: the five figures a principal wants on one screen (for the morning: open safety incidents, lessons needing cover, overdue interventions, yesterday's attendance and the amount overdue). **Full detail** shows every section. Approving, printing and deleting work the same either way. Figures that were not recorded are left off rather than shown as zero.
* **Mark as read.** When you have read a brief press **Mark as read**. It records your name and the time, and every administrator can see who has read it. Briefs you have not read yet show **Unread** on the Today page and in the history lists. A draft that is prepared again counts as unread.
* **Settings:** switch the automatic preparation on or off and set the two times. Times are in your school's time zone. Daily briefs and roll-ups are kept for **92 days (about three months)**; older ones are removed automatically so the oldest are overwritten instead of piling up. You can print or delete any of them yourself at any time.
* Preparing a brief automatically needs the school's background worker to be running. Without one (for example on a free demo host) use **Prepare it now**.

**Planning** (Reports, then **Planning**; administrators). Four parts:

* **A short written read of the figures.** Choose a report and press **Write the summary**. It describes the numbers (the figures are shown beside it) and does not explain causes or predict. Without an AI provider you get the same figures in plain words.
* **Next year's enrolment.** For each grade: this year, expected next year with a low to high range, approved offers, applications waiting, seats left and sections needed. The retention and application-conversion figures used are shown, and marked "assumed" when your school does not have enough history yet. Set seats per grade under Admissions, Waitlist.
* **Scores term over term**, by subject or by class, with a **declining** flag for a drop of 5 points or more. Groups under 10 students are never shown.
* **Staff signals.** Contracts and probation ending, credentials expiring, a lot of time away, a heavy cover load, a full timetable. These are for starting a conversation and are not a rating of anyone.

### 3.9 Import, export and restore school data (Import data)

Menu: **Import data** (admin only). Your school's data lives in one set of CSV files called a **data pack**. The same files
load data in and come out when you export, so you can move a school, restore it, or start from sample data.

The page has three tabs:

* **Load data:** choose what to do with records that already exist:
  * **Add only:** new records are added, existing ones are left alone.
  * **Add and update:** new are added, existing are updated. Empty cells never erase what is there.
  * **Restore:** for an empty school. Everything in the pack is loaded.

  Then pick a `.zip` pack (several files, loaded in the right order) or a single CSV/Excel file (say what it holds).
  You see a **preview** first: how many are New, Update, Already exists, or Needs fixing (with the row and reason).
  **Nothing is saved until you press Import.** Large packs take a few minutes; leave the page open.
* **Download:** **Export my school** gives you a zip of everything, which you can load again later. **Blank pack** gives you empty CSV files with the right column headings to fill in.
* **History:** what was loaded, when, and the result.

Files refer to each other by names people know (class name, subject code, student number, employee number), not by hidden IDs.
The columns for every file are described in `docs/DATA_PACK_SPEC.md`, and a complete sample school (Maple Grove Academy, 214
students) is in `sample-data/us-school/`.

## 4. Teachers

The teacher menu:

* **Dashboard:** today at a glance.
* **My Classes / Class Reports:** your classes, students and reports.
* **Cover & My HR:** lessons you are asked to cover (press **I can cover** if you are free and qualified), and your own HR page.
* **Safety Reports:** file an incident (injury, bullying, damage). You see the reports you filed.
* **My Interventions:** students the counselor has asked you to support. Add notes and press **Mark done** when something has been done.
* **Messages, Draft with AI:** in **New message**, pick a student, a type and a tone, add a few notes, and the AI drafts a message for you to edit. Nothing is sent until you press Send. Keep health and discipline matters for the office. In a conversation, **Summarise** also suggests a reply, and **Translate** shows a message in your language (with **Show original**).
* **Gradebook, Rubric:** on an assignment you can write a rubric or **Draft with AI**; it shows from the column heading while you grade and never changes a score. The **Lesson planner** assistant accepts standards to address.
* **Quick actions** (Attendance, Marks, Homework and so on, depending on what your school allowed you).
* **Timetable, then My Leave:** request leave.
* **Library, Messages, Announcements, Calendar, Meetings.**

Each morning: Dashboard, then mark attendance for your classes. If you are away, request leave. If a colleague is away,
check **Cover & My HR**.

## 5. Parents

* **Dashboard:** summary for all children.

## 8. Good habits

* **Check the preview** before importing; fix rows marked *Needs fixing* and load again.
* **Set up in this order:** school year and terms, classes, subjects, rooms and periods, staff (with their subjects), students and guardians, fees, then the timetable.
* **Give each teacher their subjects** under Timetable, Teaching Rules. Without them, a teacher cannot be put in a lesson or used as cover.
* **Keep one person per record.** Search before adding a student or employee so you do not create a duplicate.
* **Log out** on shared computers.

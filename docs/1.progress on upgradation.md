3.2 Trial & Billing Rules
Rule	Detail
Free Trial	30 days of Premium for every new school
Billing Cycle	Monthly or yearly (2 months free on yearly)
Upgrade	Immediate, prorated difference invoiced
Downgrade	Takes effect at end of paid period, only if usage fits
Payment Methods	Card (Stripe), bank transfer
Grace Period	7 days after due date before read-only
Read-Only Mode	View and export only; choosing a plan and paying still works
Suspension	After grace + 7 days, school suspended (data kept)
Cancellation	Keeps working until period end, then read-only; nothing deleted
Tax	Country-specific VAT/sales tax; reverse charge for tax-ID schools
Invoices	Platform invoice (PI-YYYY-NNNNN), printable, emailed
Reminders	3 days before, on due date, 3 days late, 7 days late

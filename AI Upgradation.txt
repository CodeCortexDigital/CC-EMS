CC-EMS US Upgrade Specification
Oct 9, 2026 · @code cortex in pakistan
CC-EMS needs 96 upgrades in 10 phases to go from testing to paying US private and charter schools, with the first pilots targeted for January 2027.
Overview
This document specifies every new upgrade for CC-EMS (Code Cortex Education Management System) for the US market. Each phase lists its upgrades with what changes, how we know it is done, priority and owner.
Target market: US private schools, charter schools and microschools first. Public districts come later, after SOC 2 and state reporting.
Rule for every phase: Phase 1 blocks all sales. No US school sees real data until every Critical item in Phases 1–3 is done.
Priority key
• Critical: blocks a sale or risks a privacy incident.
• High: US buyers expect it; missing it loses deals.
• Medium: improves the product; can follow the first pilots.
Owner key
• Dev: engineering team.
• Legal: policies and contracts, reviewed by a US edtech lawyer.
• Business: company setup, sales, support and partnerships.
